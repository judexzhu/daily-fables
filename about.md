<h1 class="en-only">A fable a day, and the machinery underneath it</h1>
<h1 class="zh-only zh">一日一则寓言，以及它底下的机器</h1>

<div class="en-only" markdown="1">
Every distributed-systems concept worth knowing was, at some point, a human problem first. A ledger too far from the desk. A bell that stops the room. Someone waiting for an answer that may never come.
{: .page-lede }

Daily Fables tells one of those stories each morning, in English and 中文, and then names the mechanism it was standing in for. Graduate-level material, read in five minutes, remembered because it arrived as a story.
</div>

<div class="zh-only zh" markdown="1">
每一个值得知道的分布式系统概念，最初都是一个人的问题：账房离桌子太远；一次铃响让整个大厅停摆；有人在等一个可能永远不会来的答复。
{: .page-lede }

《每日寓言》每天早上讲一个这样的故事，中英双语，然后点出它所指代的机制。研究生级别的材料，五分钟读完，因为是故事，所以记得住。
</div>
